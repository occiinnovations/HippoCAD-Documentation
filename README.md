# HippoCAD

An architectural design and structural drafting modpack for Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge). 

HippoCAD is engineered specifically for detailed builds, precision layout design, and mechanical prototyping in Minecraft. Whether you are drafting a modular city grid, designing complex interior spaces, or setting up a fully automated factory floor, this pack combines top-tier development utilities with a massive catalog of decorative blocks and cinematic tools.

## Modpack Architecture & Toolsets

### 1. Core Architectural & Drafting Tools
*   **Large-Scale Editing:** Integrates `Axiom` and `WorldEdit` for fast world-shaping, massive coordinate selections, and real-time terrain manipulation.
*   **Structure Packaging:** Uses `Buildpaste` and `Effortless Structures` to automate repetitive placement, copy-paste assets across single-player worlds, and speed up building workflows.
*   **Micro-Voxel Detailing:** Leverages `Chisel & Bits` and `Framed Blocks` to break standard 1-block constraints. This allows creators to sculpt custom geometric shapes, micro-slopes, and precision profiles.

### 2. Kinematics & Mechanical Engineering
*   **Automation Loop:** Uses the `Create` mod to design fully functional infrastructure, sliding doors, multi-tiered conveyor setups, and automated processing machinery.
*   **Rigid-Body Physics:** Integrates `Valkyrien Skies` and `Clockwork` to assign weight, physics, and continuous motion vectors to custom structures.
*   **Logistics Simulation:** Incorporates `Immersive Vehicles` alongside custom road blocks to stage ground and aerial transit networks.

### 3. Quantitative Cinematography & Rendering
*   **Hyperzoom Focal Matrix:** Stacks `Chloride` zoom and `Essential Mod` zoom to a single keybind, creating a deep focal magnification layer to frame distant details cleanly.
*   **6-DoF Camera Control:** Uses `Freecam` for decoupled camera paths and `Do a Barrel Roll` for full flight-simulator controls (pitch, yaw, roll) while flying with an elytra to capture smooth, cinematic aerial sweeps.
*   **Visual Assets:** Pre-configured with shader setups and `CompBuild Textures` to output clean, high-contrast structural captures ready for portfolio submission.

## Hardware Allocations & Requirements

Because the pack runs highly intensive rendering, custom models, and physics mods simultaneously, ensure your client runtime is configured to match these parameters:

*   **Allocated Memory (RAM):** 8GB minimum allocation required (`-Xmx8G`); 10GB or higher strongly recommended for heavy chunk loading.
*   **Graphics Computing Layer (GPU):** Dedicated mid-range card or better to run the integrated shader presets smoothly.

### Quick Start Setup
1. Download the profile directly through **Modrinth** or CurseForge.
2. Open your launcher settings and adjust the JVM arguments to allocate at least **8GB RAM**.
3. Activate the recommended `CompBuild Textures` pack in your resource menu.
4. Drop into a **Superflat world** or custom blueprint map to begin drafting your layouts.
