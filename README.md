# HippoCAD

![Hippo Logo](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f356f8d5-681f-43eb-b120-a653bdfeed23)

HippoCAD is a large, specialized Minecraft 1.20.1 (Forge) modpack built for high-detail building, city layouts, and machine automation. It combines advanced building tools with a massive library of decorative blocks, realistic vehicles, and custom physics to let you build complex projects without standard block limitations.

## What's Inside the Pack

### 1. Advanced Building Tools
* **Large-Scale Editing:** Includes `Axiom` and `WorldEdit` for fast terrain manipulation and massive selections.
* **Repetitive Building:** Uses `Buildpaste` and `Effortless Structures` to quickly copy, paste, and mirror designs.
* **Micro-Detailing:** Uses `Chisel & Bits` and `Framed Blocks` to break the standard 1-block grid, letting you sculpt micro-slopes and custom geometric shapes.

### 2. Automation & Vehicles
* **Machinery:** Features the `Create` mod for automated processing, custom moving structures, and conveyor networks.
* **Physics:** Uses `Valkyrien Skies` and `Clockwork` to add real physics, weights, and continuous motion vectors to your structures.
* **Transit Networks:** Includes `Immersive Vehicles` and dedicated road blocks to create functional ground and air traffic layouts.

### 3. Camera & Rendering Utilities
* **Focal Zoom:** Combines `Chloride` zoom and `Essential Mod` zoom on a single keybind for clean, close-up inspections of distant details.
* **Camera Movement:** Features `Freecam` and `Do a Barrel Roll` to decouple your camera path and record smooth, cinematic flight sweeps.
* **Visuals:** Pre-configured with custom shaders and the `CompBuild Textures` pack for high-contrast building captures.

## System Requirements & Setup

Because this pack runs heavy rendering engines, custom block models, and structural physics simultaneously, you must allocate enough memory to your launcher.

* **Allocated RAM:** 8GB minimum required (`-Xmx8G`); 10GB or higher is highly recommended for smooth chunk loading.
* **Graphics Card (GPU):** A dedicated mid-range graphics card is required to run the integrated shader presets.

### Quick Start Setup
1. Download the profile via **Modrinth** or **CurseForge**.
2. Open your launcher settings and ensure your JVM arguments allocate at least **8GB of RAM**.
3. Enable the recommended `CompBuild Textures` pack in your in-game resource menu.
4. Launch a **Superflat world** or a new blueprint world to start building.
